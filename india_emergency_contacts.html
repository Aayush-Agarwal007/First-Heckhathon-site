📌 Important Notes:
• 112 is the single unified emergency number — works across all states for police, fire & ambulance.
• All numbers listed as Toll Free are free to call from any mobile or landline in India.
• Some state-specific numbers may differ slightly by state/UT — contact local authorities if needed.
• In case of no network, try SMS or move to an area with signal and dial 112 immediately.
